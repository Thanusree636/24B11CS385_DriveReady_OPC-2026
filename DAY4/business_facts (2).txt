SHOPEASE ONLINE STORE — BUSINESS FACTS
Purpose: Sample business-facts file for the ShopEase Day 5 RAG exercise.
Important: This is a student demo business. Facts marked as sample/proposed are not real-world store policies.
Last prepared: 28 November 2026

A. CONFIRMED DEMO CATALOGUE FACTS
1. The demo store is named ShopEase Online Store.
2. ShopEase is presented as an online shopping store in this student project.
3. The current demo catalogue contains wireless headphones, smart watch, laptop bag, bluetooth speaker, and keyboard.
4. Wireless headphones cost Rs. 1,499 in the demo catalogue.
5. A smart watch costs Rs. 2,499 in the demo catalogue.
6. A laptop bag costs Rs. 899 in the demo catalogue.
7. A bluetooth speaker costs Rs. 1,299 in the demo catalogue.
8. A keyboard costs Rs. 699 in the demo catalogue.
9. iPhone 17 is not included in the current demo catalogue.
10. The price-checking tool is named get_product_price.
11. The delivery-checking tool is named check_delivery.
12. The order-saving tool is named log_order.

B. CONFIRMED DEMO DELIVERY AND ORDER FACTS
13. The demo delivery tool currently accepts Hyderabad.

15. The demo delivery tool currently accepts Vijayawada.
16. The demo delivery tool currently accepts Visakhapatnam.
17. The demo delivery tool currently accepts Rajahmundry.
18. The demo delivery tool currently accepts Kakinada.
19. The supported payment methods in the order model are UPI, card, and cash on delivery.
20. An order must have a customer name, catalogue product, quantity of at least 1, delivery date, city, and payment method.
21. The agent is instructed to show an order summary and ask for customer confirmation before saving.
22. The log_order tool validates order fields and calculates the total using the demo catalogue price.
23. Successful demo orders are appended to the ShopEase Customer Orders Google Sheet.
24. The Google Sheet columns are customer_name, product_name, quantity, delivery_date, city, payment_method, and total_price.

RAG ANSWERING GUIDANCE
If a customer asks about a fact listed above, answer from this file.
If a customer asks about a policy marked not configured, say that the information is not currently available in the demo store and do not invent an answer.
Do not claim that a real payment, shipment, refund, email, or external transaction has occurred. The current project demonstrates order logging to Google Sheets only.
